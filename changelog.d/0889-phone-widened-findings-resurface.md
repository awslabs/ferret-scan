### ⚠️ Breaking Changes

- **phone (suppression rules for widened numbers):** a PHONE finding that now covers the whole
  number has a different text from the one it had, so a suppression rule written for the shorter
  span no longer matches it and the finding is reported again: `+1 297 201 0052` (was
  `297 201 0052`), `1-888-201-0055` (`888-201-0055`), `297-201-0056 ext 27` (`297-201-0056`),
  `+1 (297) 201-0054`, `+39 310 101 1057` and `+44 (0)161 496 0815` (whose tails were US_Dashed
  findings), `+44 207 946 0372` (two findings become one), and `00CC`/`+CC` numbers that were cut
  after a group (`0033 1 99 00 47 18`, `+55 11 91010-1050`, `+49 30 23125-417`). Measured with
  rules generated by the previous release and enabled: of its 52 findings on the issue's
  reproduction set, the 24 that keep their text are still suppressed and counted, the 28 widened
  ones resurface. A finding the E.164 gate caps at LOW keeps matching rules written by the current
  formula, which leaves confidence out, but no longer matches a legacy v1 rule, which hashes it.
  New findings (`tel:` URIs, vCard values, display forms) can make a scan that was clean exit
  non-zero. Review the resurfaced findings and regenerate their rules with
  `--generate-suppressions` ([#889](https://github.com/awslabs/ferret-scan/issues/889)).
